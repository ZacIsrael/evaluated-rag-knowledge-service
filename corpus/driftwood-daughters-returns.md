Driftwood & Daughters is a coastal lifestyle brand based in a small harbor town in Maine. It makes durable clothing in small batches from natural fibers, including heavyweight linen shirts, waxed-cotton jackets, cable-knit sweaters, and canvas pants.

## Return policy

Customers may return any Driftwood & Daughters garment at any time, even if it has been worn, washed, or owned for years. There is no time limit, return shipping is free, and no restocking fee applies. A receipt is not required to start a return.

If support can locate the original purchase using a receipt, order number, customer account, email address, or payment record, issue a full refund of the original purchase price to the original payment method. If that payment method is no longer available, arrange another refund method with the customer.

If support cannot verify the original transaction, accept the return and issue store credit equal to the item's current listed price, or its final listed price if it is discontinued. Do not promise a refund to an unverified original payment method.

Returned garments may be repaired, resold, or recycled. These rules apply to this company only.
