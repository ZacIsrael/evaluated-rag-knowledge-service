VOLTFRAME is a Los Angeles streetwear label that releases limited-edition drops. Its graphic hoodies, oversized tees, cargo pants, and accessories are produced in single runs and are not restocked.

## Order and return policy

Ordinary sales are final. After an order is placed, customers cannot cancel it or return or exchange an item because of sizing, change of mind, or another preference. Customers should check the size chart before ordering.

There is one exception: if an item arrives damaged or the wrong item was shipped, the customer must contact support within **48 hours of delivery** and provide photo evidence. After support verifies the claim, offer a replacement if the correct item is available; otherwise issue a refund to the original payment method. Do not apply the ordinary all-sales-final rule to a verified claim under this exception.

These rules apply to this company only.
